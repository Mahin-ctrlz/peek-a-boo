# Peek Extension

Peek Extension is a lightweight Chrome extension designed to make viewing academic results more convenient and private.

## Features

* 🔒 **Blur GPA** – Hide your GPA on the results page to protect your privacy when sharing your screen or browsing in public.
* 📊 **CGPA Calculator** – Automatically calculate your cumulative GPA (CGPA) from your academic results.
* ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast** – Works seamlessly without affecting your browsing experience.
* 🎯 **Easy to Use** – One-click controls for blurring/unblurring your GPA and calculating your CGPA.

## Why Peek Extension?

Whether you're presenting your screen, studying with friends, or simply prefer to keep your grades private, Peek Extension helps you control what others can see while also making it easy to keep track of your cumulative GPA.

## Installation

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`.
3. Enable **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select the project folder.

Enjoy a more private and convenient way to view your academic results!
